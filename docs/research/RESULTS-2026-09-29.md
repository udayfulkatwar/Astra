# Results — LSFVG v1.0 on genuine history, 2010–2019 (2026-09-29)

**Outcome: no edge. Nothing is selected for trading.** Per `PROTOCOL-2026-09-29.md` (committed
in `f9f03cf`, before any result existed), neither model of the owner's strategy passes. None of
the 14 in-sample candidates of Stage 2 has a positive expectancy either. This is Stage 3: no
strategy is recommended for trading, and no further searching was done on this data.

- **Data:** HistData M1 bid prices, EUR/USD · GBP/USD · USD/JPY, 2010-01-03 → 2019-06-21
  (`data-histdata-2010-2019.md`).
- **Rules:** LSFVG v1.0 exactly as frozen (ADR-0024).
- **Replay:** ASTRA's real gate in a strategy study (ADR-0025): 0.25 % risk, the owner's limits,
  flat before the weekend.
- **Costs:** an assumed 0.8 pip spread, 7 USD per lot commission, 2 ticks of stop slippage, and
  limit fills only 1 tick through the limit.

Full reports and trade lists:

- `lsfvg-v1-histdata-2010-2019.md` (ASTRA's report);
- `lsfvg-v1-histdata-2010-2019-trades-A.csv` and `lsfvg-v1-histdata-2010-2019-trades-B.csv`.

## Why (what the numbers show)

- **Almost no raw edge.** Before any cost, Model A makes +0.02 R per trade and Model B loses.
  Costs take about 0.13 R per trade. The median stop is about 17 pips, so the commission alone
  is 0.05 R, and the assumed 0.8-pip spread and the slippage add the rest.
- **Half the orders never fill.** Of 908 approved LIMIT orders (Model A), 471 expired unfilled.
  The retraces the strategy waits for often do not come, and the losing trades are more often
  the ones that do fill.
- **No stable period.** Model A was positive only in 2015, 2016 and 2018. By pair it was flat on
  USD/JPY (+0.002 R per trade) and negative on EUR/USD (−0.12 R) and GBP/USD (−0.20 R).
- **The gate was not the cause.** It refused only 58 of 966 setups (market close, limits,
  weekend).

## Verification

- ASTRA's research CLI and the standalone Python kit ran the same data independently. They
  produced **identical** trades: 437 and 363, every field equal, and the same ending balances.
- The 2010–2016 figures come from a separate in-sample-only run; they match ASTRA's
  in-sample / out-of-sample split.

## Limits of this evidence

- The data is bid-only, so the spread is assumed. With raw spreads of 0.1–0.3 pip the cost drag
  would be smaller, but Model A before costs is still only +0.02 R per trade: nothing to trade.
- The period is 2010–2019, not the SPEC's 2020–2026, which remains untested. No historical news
  calendar was available (the news filter was not modelled). Instrument specs are UNVERIFIED
  templates.
- A strategy study, not a prop-firm evaluation.

## Decision

LSFVG v1.0 is **not recommended for real money**. It stays available in ASTRA for paper
trading only. Live trading needs the owner's six factors (ADR-0008); this result is one more
reason not to seek them for this strategy.

Next evidence that could change this:

- the SPEC's 2020–2026 period, run with the standalone kit or with Dukascopy allowed;
- different strategy rules from the owner, tested under a new protocol before any result is
  seen.
