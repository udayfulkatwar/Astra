# Research protocol — written before any result was seen (2026-09-29)

The owner delegated the choice of strategy to Claude ("I will give you authority to choose the
strategy or any model"). This protocol fixes, **before** the first genuine-history result
exists, how that choice is made. It is committed before the results so the order can be
checked in Git.

## Data

HistData.com M1 bid prices for EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY, 2010-01-01 → 2019-06-21. They come
from the public repository `philipperemy/fx-1-minute-data` at commit
`8bcf4a752e4777edd3b3e4c29221eb974d7affc6`; provenance is in `data-histdata-2010-2019.md`.

- There is no ask data. The spread is ASSUMED at 0.8 pip on every pair, and the sensitivity
  runs cover 1.2 and 1.6 pip.
- This is not the SPEC's 2020–2026 period. That period stays **untouched**: it is the final
  confirmation once its data is available.

## Stage 1 — the owner's strategy, unchanged

LSFVG v1.0 runs as frozen in ADR-0024 / `config/strategies/lsfvg-{a,b}.yaml`, with no parameter
changes, through ASTRA's research replay (strategy study, costs as ADR-0025).

- Window: 2010-01-01 → 2019-06-22.
- In-sample: before 2017-01-01. Out-of-sample: 2017-01-01 onwards.

A model counts as **showing an edge** only if **all** of these hold, after costs:

1. at least 100 trades over the whole window, and at least 30 out-of-sample;
2. expectancy > 0 R both in-sample and out-of-sample;
3. profit factor ≥ 1.10 over the whole window;
4. positive expectancy in at least 60 % of the 6-month walk-forward windows that have trades;
5. Monte Carlo (bootstrap of trade R): the 5th percentile of total R is > 0;
6. expectancy stays > 0 with the spread × 1.5 and with 5 ticks of slippage.

If one model passes, it is selected. If both pass, the one with the higher **in-sample**
expectancy is selected.

## Stage 2 — only if neither model passes

The candidates are the 11 pre-defined sensitivity variants of each model (22 candidates) plus
the two base models: 24 in all. No other variant is added.

1. Each candidate runs on the in-sample window only (2010-01-01 → 2017-01-01).
2. The candidate with the highest in-sample expectancy among those with ≥ 100 in-sample trades
   is chosen. The cost variants (spread, slippage, fill rules) are measurements, not strategies,
   so they are not eligible; the eligible ones are the 8 parameter variants and the 2 base
   models.
3. That single candidate runs **once** on 2017-01-01 → 2019-06-22. It is selected only if it
   meets criteria 1–6 on that period, with criterion 1 reading "≥ 30 trades".
4. The report states that 10 candidates were compared (a multiple-testing risk).

## Stage 3 — if nothing is selected

No strategy is recommended for trading. That is reported plainly, and no further searching
happens on this data. Next steps: the 2020–2026 data, or strategy rules from the owner.

## Whatever is selected

- It goes to **paper trading only**. Live trading needs the owner's six live factors
  (ADR-0008); this protocol never enables it.
- A selected strategy is confirmed on 2020–2026 data before any live consideration.
- Prop-firm pass/fail is not claimed until the owner's firm rules are entered and verified.
